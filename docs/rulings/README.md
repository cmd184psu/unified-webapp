# Rulings

`RULINGS.md` holds the FINAL answers to "which behaviour is right here?". Only a ruling is authority: the owner's, or the coordinator's strictly under the owner's rules.
The owner can overrule anything (including the C# oracle tests and civ3.exe research) at any time. openciv3 (the C# fork) and civ3.exe research are both incomplete: they are
EVIDENCE that informs a ruling, never a master; a ruling's `source` column says what its basis is.

Workers and reviewers: read RULINGS.md BEFORE guessing. If your task text, a note or a review demand conflicts with a ruling, the ruling wins; say so in your report instead of looping.
No ruling for your question? Follow the plan text; if it is silent, choose the simplest behaviour that satisfies it, say so in your report, and do not block on research.
Do not edit these files (generated from the coordinator's database); report a missing or wrong ruling in your DONE report.
